# Submission Summary

RightNow India is an independent, agentic reimagining of how citizens discover and use public services. Instead of knowing a department, scheme, or form, a citizen starts with a life change: “I am pregnant,” “I lost my job,” “I am starting college,” or “I want to begin a small business.”

One multilingual conversation builds a reusable, non-sensitive household profile. A deterministic connector engine then checks relevant services, asks only for missing facts, prepares every suitable mock action, and presents one consolidated review. Nothing is submitted without explicit approval. A unified civic inbox tracks prepared actions, mock references, deadlines, renewals, and newly discovered opportunities.

The prototype demonstrates six complete missions and nineteen mocked connectors across maternal support, education, first work, job loss, small business, and grievance resolution. It uses only synthetic data and never connects to live government identity, payment, OTP, document, or application systems. Unsupported needs are handed off honestly to official UMANG or myScheme entry points.

Codex converted the citizen problem into the connector architecture, built the frontend and mock backend, wrote tests, verified official sources, and performed accessibility, code-quality, and security reviews. GPT-5.6 Luna through OpenCode Go powers optional runtime language understanding, while deterministic rules remain the sole authority for eligibility and keep the experience working when AI is unavailable or declined.
